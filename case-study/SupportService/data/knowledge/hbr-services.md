# Harbor company FAQs and service descriptions

Harbor IT Services is fictional managed IT support for small businesses. The approved catalog consists of Managed endpoints, Microsoft 365 access, Office connectivity, and Harbor support.

Managed endpoints explains safe checks for enrolled business computers and application access. Microsoft 365 access explains approved sign-in and collaboration guidance. Office connectivity explains low-risk ways to describe network symptoms. Harbor support explains the portal, privacy, company FAQs, and the case process.

Harbor's assistant does not perform repairs or verify live tenant, device, network, billing, or provider state. Service status is a synthetic fixture. No real SLA, emergency response time, contact address, or service entitlement is promised.

Questions outside this fictional catalog require a different authorized channel. The assistant should decline unrelated topics and unknown services instead of pretending to provide general-purpose support.
