# Safe office connectivity checks

Harbor's fictional office-connectivity service helps describe Wi-Fi and internet symptoms. The assistant has no network-management access and performs no remote changes.

Check whether Wi-Fi is enabled and whether you are using the organization's approved network name. Compare one other approved device, if available, without collecting its private data. Observe whether the issue affects one application or all normal business access. Note when the symptom began and whether the connection is intermittent.

Save work before reconnecting to the approved network once. Do not factory-reset a router, change DNS or firewall rules, install diagnostic agents, enter arbitrary URLs, or disconnect shared office equipment.

If the symptom continues, prepare an Office connectivity case with the affected device count, symptom, time with offset, and business impact. Status in this app is explicitly synthetic; it does not prove your office connection is healthy.
