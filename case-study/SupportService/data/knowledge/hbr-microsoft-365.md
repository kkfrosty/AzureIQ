# Microsoft 365 sign-in and access guidance

Harbor's fictional Microsoft 365 service provides safe sign-in and collaboration guidance. It is not a Microsoft outage feed and cannot reset a password, change MFA, unlock an account, or grant access.

Use your organization's already-approved sign-in entry point. Check the displayed account and application name without sharing the account address here. Confirm the device clock and time zone are sensible. Close a duplicate sign-in tab and try the approved flow once. Follow your organization's existing MFA and self-service recovery instructions; never approve an unexpected MFA prompt.

For Teams, Outlook, or another approved Microsoft 365 app, note whether the issue affects one app or several, and record the non-sensitive error code and time. Do not send passwords, verification codes, tokens, or screenshots containing private information.

If approved sign-in guidance does not resolve the symptom, prepare a Microsoft 365 access case. Harbor cannot claim that it checked your tenant or repaired your account.
